The moderate temperatures in the Highland Lakes allows for outdoor activities year-round, including dining out, thus a nice selection of restaurants with decks or patios. Many are dog-friendly, although some might have restrictions. If in doubt, call ahead or check online.

Here’s a list of Highland Lakes eateries that welcome canine clientele.

MARBLE FALLS

Adele’s Restaurant
112 Hwy 281, 830-637-7123

Adele’s Big Bone Patio has an icehouse atmosphere offering a relaxed atmosphere for guests and their dogs. Pizzas, pastas, sticky ribs, and more are on the menu at Adele’s. Sip on cocktails, beer, wine, mocktails while watching the game with your furry best friend.

Bear King Brewing Company
207 Avenue G, 830-220-5083

Chill out on Bear King’s inviting patio. Order a house-brewed beer and something from its comfort food-centric menu. Online reviewers give a thumbs-up to dog-friendly staff. 

Double Horn Brewing Company
208 Avenue H, 830-693-5165

Join other two-legged and four-legged patrons on Double Horn’s spacious, Fido-friendly patio for craft brews and hand-formed burgers.

Rae’s Rbar & Grill
904 Third St., 830-693-2622

“Water bowl … treats … a friendly hello and really good food,” reports one online reviewer. Rae’s has a doggie menu and hosts pet adoptions twice a year.

BURNET

Bill’s Burgers, Wings, & Things
306 W. Polk St., 512-234-8216
(Also a Lampasas location at 904 N. Key Ave.)

The name tells you what headlines the menu, and the large outdoor space has plenty of room for you and your doggie friend (on a leash, please).

KINGSLAND

Wakepoint LBJ
14757 RR 1431 West, 325-484-4448

Arrive by land or water at this Lake LBJ restaurant/bar/entertainment venue. Find an outdoor table for you and your favorite hound, pick something from the extensive menu, and take in the waterfront views.

COTTONWOOD SHORES

LeStourgeon Seafood Company
4320 FM 2147, 830-265-6116

This popular Horseshoe Bay-area restaurant has a Cajun/seafood menu, strong margaritas, and a covered front porch for you and your pooch. 

JOHNSON CITY

Pecan Street Brewing
106 E. Pecan Drive, 830-868-2500

Enjoy artisan beers, a comfort food menu, a shaded biergarten, and dog-loving staff. The biergarten is sometimes booked for private parties. Call ahead.

This is not an all-inclusive list, so if you don’t see your favorite dog-friendly place here, please share it with our readers in the comments below.
